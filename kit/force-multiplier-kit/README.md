# FORCE MULTIPLIER
## Ethical AI

Upload **your** 3 files. Paste 1 prompt. Any AI tool.

Not our files. Not a template. Not a fake expert pack.
The files are whatever already runs your work.

---

## Do this

1. Pick 3 files you already use in your craft.
2. Upload them to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Cursor, or a local model.
3. Paste the operator prompt from `OPERATOR_PROMPTS.md`.
4. Drop the new job on top (drawings, P&L, brief, photos, messy client text).

The model is told: you are this person's Senior Apprentice. Files beat textbook knowledge.

---

## What counts as “your 3 files”

Things on your machine today. Mixed formats are fine.

| Usually | Examples of files you already have |
|---|---|
| How you decide | Notes, voice transcript, email you sent a junior, “I don’t bid X” list |
| How you do the work | Bid workbook, FP&A model, takeoff sheet, costing sheet, estimate template, SOP |
| Proof | A quote you would sign again, a closed model, a marked-up plan, a finished job file |

One real spreadsheet + two nearby scraps is enough.
Do not invent a third file to make the number tidy.
If you have fifty files, pick the three you would hand a new hire on day one.

See `WHAT_TO_UPLOAD.md` for industry examples. Those are examples of *file types*, not files we wrote for you.
