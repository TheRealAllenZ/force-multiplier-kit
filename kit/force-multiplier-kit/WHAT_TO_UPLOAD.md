# WHAT TO UPLOAD
Your files. From your industry. Already on your machine.

This page names *types of files people already have*.
It is not a pack we wrote for you. Do not download these as content.

---

## How to pick

Hand a new hire three things on day one. Those three.

Prefer:

- The workbook or template you actually price / model / spec from
- One finished job you would still sign
- The notes that live in your head (email, voice dump, “never do this” list)

Avoid:

- The whole archive
- A file you would not show a junior
- Live unpublished bids you are still competing on, medical identifiers, account numbers, anything that sinks you if it leaks
- A blank template you downloaded from the internet and never used

If the model needs a number that is only in your head, type that number into the chat. Do not hope the model invents it.

---

## File types by craft (examples only)

**Estimator / construction / trades**
- Bid workbook or takeoff sheet
- Exclusions / clarifications you reuse
- One past quote + the drawing or schedule it came from

**FP&A / operator finance**
- The live model (`.xlsx`)
- Last variance writeup or board page
- Close checklist or calendar notes

**Designer / video / photo**
- Brand or spec notes you actually follow
- One finished file you still like
- Export settings / size / color-space notes

**Chef / shop costing**
- Recipe or BOM sheet with your yields
- Vendor / plate-cost file
- One menu or job you would run again

**Service / diagnostic (diesel, HVAC, appliances)**
- The diagnostic checklist you actually use
- A writeup of a job the scan tool lied on
- Parts-and-labor sheet in your layout

**Dispatcher / shop foreman**
- Board rules you already wrote down
- A day-sheet that worked
- The message you send customers about ETAs

If your craft is not listed: same test. What three files would you hand a new hire.

---

## Formats that work

`.xlsx` `.xls` `.csv` `.pdf` `.docx` `.txt` `.md` `.png` `.jpg`

Spreadsheets and plain text beat 200-page scanned PDFs.
A photo of a marked drawing is fine if that is how you work.
A voice transcript pasted into a `.txt` is a file.

---

## Redact before you upload

Strip names that did not consent, account numbers, private addresses if that is a problem, medical IDs, unpublished live bid totals.

Keep the structure, the counts, the labels, the rhythm. That is what the model needs.
